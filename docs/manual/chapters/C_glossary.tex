\chapter{Glossary and symbols}\label{app:glossary}
\begin{description}
\item[Allowance] The chosen monthly amount for goals and extra debt funding through the priority ladder.
\item[Anchor] An observed balance and its date used to reconstruct a ledger backward and forward.
\item[Available before goals] Budget income less the specified spending, regular debt, reserve, and investing lines, before subtracting the allowance.
\item[Bootstrap] Repeated sampling from recorded observations to construct a modeled distribution or estimate an interval.
\item[Cadence] An item's repeat interval, such as weekly, every two weeks, semimonthly, or monthly.
\item[Checkpoint] A saved summary and forecast used for later comparison. It is not a restorable database backup.
\item[Complete month or week] An elapsed calendar period eligible under the particular calculation. Monthly averages and weekly forecast samples use different missing-period rules.
\item[Deterministic reference] A calculation without stochastic draws. It is not automatically the stochastic mean, median, or most likely complete path.
\item[Earmark] An explicit assignment of existing holdings to a savings purpose. It does not create another asset or a bank transfer.
\item[Effective sample size] The information-equivalent count used by the implemented weighting formula, \(1/\sum w_j^2\) for normalized weights.
\item[Goal progress] Savings still held plus recorded qualifying purchases for a savings goal; a household-balance allocation for a cash or net-worth milestone.
\item[Located savings] Recorded goal savings assigned to a specific tracked account.
\item[Net worth] Assets less debt as represented by the relevant history or model. Compare the components where an implementation qualification applies.
\item[Override] A deliberate replacement of a detected budget amount. A group-total override takes precedence over category overrides inside that group.
\item[Pending] An unposted entry supplied by an export. It is kept outside the posted ledger and is not a live reservation of all possible bank holds.
\item[Percentile] A pointwise rank in modeled outcomes. P50 is the median at a date; a sequence of percentile values need not be one simulated trajectory.
\item[Permutation comparison] A comparison constructed by exchanging observation labels under an exchangeability premise.
\item[Personal amount] The part of a transaction retained after applicable business or reimbursement exclusions for personal analysis.
\item[Posted close] The account balance after all postings for the selected date, as distinguished from an available balance affected by holds.
\item[Provenance] The retained origin of an item or result, including source records, classification evidence, or generation inputs and hashes.
\item[Release] A goal activity that removes saved money and progress so that its use no longer counts toward the goal.
\item[Rollover] Reallocation after a target finishes, including regular loan payments freed after payoff.
\item[Scenario] A conditional set of changes to a baseline model, such as a spending multiplier or hypothetical dated expense.
\item[Unassigned holding] Tracked funding capacity not yet claimed by recorded savings goals. This is distinct from recorded savings whose account has not been assigned.
\item[Unlocated savings] Already-counted goal savings without an assigned account. Locating it changes attribution rather than progress.
\item[Weighted estimate] An average that gives observations different specified influence rather than treating them all equally.
\end{description}
